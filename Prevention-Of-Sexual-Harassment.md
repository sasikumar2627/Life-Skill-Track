## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Sending or showing inappropriate messages, images, or videos.
- Unwanted physical contact such as touching, hugging, or brushing against someone.
- Asking for sexual favors or making promises of benefits in exchange for them.
- Repeatedly asking someone out even after they say no.
- Making gestures, or behaving in a way that makes someone feel uncomfortable.
- Spreading rumors or making comments about someone's appearance or personal life in a sexual way.

## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- **Clearly tell the person to stop** if I feel safe doing so.
- **Document the incident** by noting the date, time, place, and details of what happened.
- **Report the incident** to the appropriate authority, such as the HR team or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
- **Support the affected person** if I witness such behavior and encourage them to report it.
- **Follow the company’s PoSH policy and procedures** for handling complaints.
