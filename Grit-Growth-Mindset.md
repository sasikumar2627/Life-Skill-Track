## GRIT

Psychologist Angela Lee Duckworth explains that long-term success comes more from sustained passion and perseverance—what she calls grit—than from talent or IQ, and that developing a growth mindset helps people stick with their goals over time.

## Introduction to Growth Mindset

Difference between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset, showing how people who believe abilities can be developed through effort learn more and grow, while those who see abilities as fixed limit their own potential.

## Understanding Internal Locus of Control

Internal Locus of Control means believing that your own actions, effort and decisions are what shape the results in your life, rather than depending on luck, fate, or external circumstances. The main point of the video is that when you take responsibility for your outcomes and focus on what you can control, you stay more motivated and are better able to direct your life and goals.

## How to build a Growth Mindset
Here are the key points the speaker mentions for building a growth mindset (short list only, no explanations):

1 **Believe in your ability to figure things out.**

2 **Question your negative assumptions.**

3 **Create your own curriculum for skill development.**

4 **Honor the struggle.**

## What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

Here are some practical ideas to build a growth mindset:

1 **Set small but challenging goals and celebrate progress rather than perfection.**

2 **mbrace failure as feedback — treat mistakes as learning opportunities.**

3 **Practice self-reflection daily to notice fixed-mindset thoughts and reframe them.**

4 **Seek out new skills or experiences that push you slightly outside your comfort zone.**

5 **Ask for feedback and use it to improve rather than take it as judgment.**
