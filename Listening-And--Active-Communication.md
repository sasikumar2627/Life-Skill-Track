# What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. **Pay Full Attention**
   - Eliminate distractions like phones or laptops and focus completely on the speaker.

2. **Maintain Eye Contact**
   - Shows interest and helps you stay engaged in the conversation.

3. **Avoid Interrupting**
   - Let the speaker finish their thoughts before responding.

4. **Use Verbal and Non-Verbal Cues**
   - Nod, smile, or use short responses like *“I see”* or *“Go on”* to show understanding.

5. **Listen Without Judging**
   - Keep an open mind and avoid forming opinions before the speaker finishes.

6. **Ask Relevant Questions**
   - Ask thoughtful questions to gain clarity and show genuine interest.

7. **Respond Appropriately**
   - Give honest, respectful, and well-thought-out responses after listening fully.


# According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. **Listen Carefully**
   - Give full attention to the speaker and try to understand their message clearly.

2. **Understand Feelings and Meaning**
   - Focus not only on the words, but also on the emotions behind them.

3. **Restate What You Heard**
   - Repeat the speaker’s ideas in your own simple words to show understanding.

4. **Do Not Add Opinions**
   - Avoid giving advice, judging, or sharing your own views while reflecting.

5. **Check for Accuracy**
   - Ask or confirm if your understanding of the speaker is correct.

6. **Show Empathy**
   - Respond in a caring and respectful way so the speaker feels heard and valued.

# What are the obstacles in your listening process?

1. **Distractions**
   - Noise, mobile phones, or other activities can take attention away from listening.

2. **Lack of Interest**
   - When the topic feels boring or unimportant, the mind tends to wander.

3. **Prejudices and Assumptions**
   - Judging the speaker or topic in advance can block true understanding.

4. **Emotional Reactions**
   - Strong emotions like anger, stress, or excitement can prevent careful listening.

5. **Interrupting the Speaker**
   - Thinking about what to say next instead of listening fully.

# What can you do to improve your listening?

1. **Give Full Attention**
   - I will focus completely on the speaker and avoid distractions like my phone.

2. **Maintain Eye Contact**
   - This helps me stay attentive and shows that I am interested.

3. **Avoid Interrupting**

4. **Listen With an Open Mind**
   - I will avoid judging or making assumptions while listening.

5. **Ask Questions**
   - I will ask relevant questions to clarify and better understand the message.

6. **Paraphrase What I Hear**
   - I will repeat the main points in my own words to confirm understanding.

7. **Control Emotions**
   - I will stay calm and not let emotions block my listening.

8. **Practice Regularly**
   - I will practice active listening in daily conversations to improve over time.

# When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

1. **When I Want to Avoid Conflict**
   - I stay quiet to keep peace and avoid arguments.

2. **When the Situation Is Not Important**
   - I do not express my opinion if the matter does not affect me much.

3. **When Talking to Seniors or Elders**
   - I may listen more and speak less out of respect.

4. **When I Feel Unsure or Unconfident**
   - I avoid speaking up if I am not sure about my thoughts.

5. **When I Am Tired or Stressed**
   - I may not have the energy to explain my feelings.

6. **When the Other Person Is Angry**
   - I stay passive to calm the situation and prevent escalation.

# When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

1. **When I Feel Very Angry**
   - Strong emotions make me speak loudly or harshly.

2. **When Someone Disrespects Me**
   - I react strongly to defend myself.

3. **When I Feel Treated Unfairly**
   - I become aggressive to make my point clear.

4. **When I Am Under Heavy Stress**
   - Pressure can cause impatience and rude responses.

5. **When Someone Does Not Listen Repeatedly**
   - I raise my voice to get attention.

6. **When I Feel My Limits Are Crossed**
   - I speak forcefully to set boundaries.

# When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

1. **When I Feel Angry but Don’t Want to Fight**
   - I hide my anger instead of saying it directly.

2. **When I Feel Ignored or Not Valued**
   - I may use sarcasm or silent treatment to show my frustration.

3. **When I Am Afraid of Direct Confrontation**
   - I express my feelings indirectly through taunts or gossip.

4. **When I Feel Hurt or Disappointed**
   - Instead of talking openly, I show it through negative behavior.

5. **When I Want to Avoid Responsibility**
   - I agree outwardly but show resistance through actions or tone.

6. **When I Feel Powerless**
   - Passive-aggressive behavior feels like a safe way to express anger.

# How can you make your communication assertive?

1. **Express My Thoughts Clearly**
   - I will say what I think and feel in a simple and direct way.

2. **Use “I” Statements**
   - I will say things like *“I feel…”* or *“I need…”* instead of blaming others.

3. **Stay Calm and Respectful**
   - I will control my tone and body language, even when I disagree.

4. **Listen Actively**
   - I will listen to the other person fully before responding.

5. **Set Clear Boundaries**
   - I will clearly say yes or no without feeling guilty.

6. **Avoid Aggressive or Passive Behavior**
   - I will stand up for myself without shouting or staying silent.

7. **Practice Confidence**
   - I will maintain eye contact and speak with confidence.

8. **Practice Regularly**
   - I will apply assertive communication in daily situations like at home, college, or work.
