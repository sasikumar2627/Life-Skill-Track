**Most Interesting Idea:**  
Fogg highlights that *tiny habits* — very small, easy-to-do behaviors — can gradually lead to major, lasting change when consistently started and linked to existing routines.

## Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message
### How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

**How to use B = MAP to make forming new habits easier:**  
Use the Fogg Behavior Model by ensuring that a desired behavior only happens when *Motivation (M)*, *Ability (A)*, and a *Prompt (P)* come together — if any element is missing or weak, the habit won’t get started.

- **M = Motivation**: Your desire or drive to perform the behavior.
- **A = Ability**: How easy or simple the behavior is to do (lower effort increases ability).
- **P = Prompt**: A cue or trigger that reminds you to act at the right moment.

By making the behavior easier (increasing Ability), boosting your motivation when needed, and placing consistent prompts, you can reliably initiate and repeat new habits.

## Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating immediately after completing a habit wires the behavior into your brain by creating a positive emotion.

- Celebration releases dopamine, which reinforces the behavior.
- The brain remembers what feels good and wants to repeat it.
- Emotion (not repetition alone) is what makes habits stick.
- Even tiny celebrations (smile, say “Yes!”, small fist pump) strengthen the habit loop.
- Whatever you celebrate, your brain labels as “worth repeating” — and that’s how habits are formed.

## Book Summary of Atomic Habits

### What is the book's perspective about Identity?

**Book’s Perspective on Identity:**  
The book teaches that lasting habit change comes from reshaping *your identity*, not just setting goals — every small habit you repeat acts like a “vote” for the kind of person you want to become, gradually building a new self-image that makes the habit stick.

### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

**Making a Habit Easier to Do:**  
The book emphasizes reducing the friction between you and the habit by simplifying and structuring your environment so that the behavior requires less effort. Strategies include:

- **Optimize your environment** to make the desired habit obvious and accessible.  
- **Reduce steps and obstacles** that stand between you and the habit.  
- **Use the “Two-Minute Rule”** — start with a habit that takes only two minutes, making it easy to begin.  
- **Automate decisions** so choosing the habit becomes effortless.  
- **Prepare cues and tools ahead of time** to reduce the effort needed when the moment comes.

By making a habit simple and easy, it increases your ability to perform it consistently — and consistency is the key to long-term success.

### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

**Making a Habit Harder to Do:**  
To break a bad habit, the book advises increasing the effort and friction required so you’re less likely to act on it. Key strategies include:

- **Increase friction** by adding steps or obstacles between you and the bad habit.  
- **Remove cues** that trigger the unwanted behavior.  
- **Make access difficult** — hide or store temptations out of reach or out of sight.  
- **Use commitment devices** to lock yourself into better choices.  
- **Design your environment against the habit** so automatic impulses are harder to follow.

By making bad habits less convenient and more effortful, your brain is less likely to default to them, helping you reduce or eliminate those behaviors over time.

## Reflection
### Pick one habit that you would like to do more of? What are the steps that you can take to make it make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and or response satisfying?
### 1. Make the Cue Obvious
- Keep a book on my study table.
- Set a fixed time (10 PM) for reading.
- Put a reminder on my phone.

### 2. Make It Attractive
- Choose books I truly enjoy.
- Read in a comfortable place.
- Pair reading with a cup of tea.

### 3. Make It Easy
- Start with just 5 minutes if I feel lazy.
- Keep the book ready with a bookmark.
- Read one small section at a time.

### 4. Make It Satisfying
- Track reading days on a calendar.
- Reward myself after finishing a chapter.
- Feel proud and say “Good job!” after reading.


## Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make it make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?
### 1. Make the Cue Invisible
- Remove social media apps from home screen.
- Turn off notifications.
- Keep my phone in another room while studying.

### 2. Make It Unattractive
- Remind myself it wastes my time.
- Think about my goals before opening the app.
- Follow only useful or educational pages.

### 3. Make It Hard
- Log out after every use.
- Set app time limits.
- Delete the app during exam time.

### 4. Make It Unsatisfying
- Track how much time I waste.
- Tell a friend my goal so I feel accountable.
- Replace scrolling with reading or exercise.
