## What is Deep work

- **Deep Work** is a way of working where you **focus deeply on one task without distractions**.
- It means working with **full concentration**, not checking phones, social media.
- During deep work, your brain works at **its best level** on difficult or important tasks.
- This kind of focus helps you **learn hard things faster** and **produce high-quality work**.
- Deep Work is **becoming rare but very valuable** in a world full of distractions.

## How to do deep work properly?

**1. Schedule focused time**
- Block out specific chunks in your calendar for deep work.
- Make sure this time is *distraction-free* (no phone, emails, apps).

**2. Protect your attention**
- Turn off notifications and silence interruptions.

**3. Use routines and rituals**
- Choose a quiet place.
- Decide how long you will work.
- Have everything ready before you start.

**4. Rest your brain**
- Deep work is tiring — take breaks and fully rest afterward.
- Good rest helps you focus better next time.

**5. Practice regularly**
- You get better at deep work with practice.
- Aim for a few focused sessions each week.

## How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Start my day with focus
- Remove distractions
- Set clear goals
- Use deadlines
- Take proper breaks
- Be consistent

## What are the dangers of social media?

**1. Reduces Focus**
- Constant notifications break concentration.
- Makes deep work and serious thinking difficult.

**2. Wastes Time**
- Easy to scroll for hours without realizing.
- Reduces time for meaningful work and learning.

**3. Increases Anxiety & Stress**
- Comparing your life with others can lower self-confidence.
- Fear of missing out (FOMO) creates mental pressure.

**4. Weakens Real Relationships**
- Online interactions may replace real, deep conversations.
- Can reduce quality time with family and friends.

**5. Information Overload**
- Too much content overwhelms the brain.
- Makes it harder to think clearly.
