# What is the Feynman Technique?

**Feynman Technique** is a learning method where you understand a topic by explaining it in very simple words as if you are teaching it to someone else.

# What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Active (Focused) Mode of Thinking
- This mode is used when you are **concentrating hard on a problem**.
- It is **logical, step-by-step, and analytical**.
- Example: Solving a math problem, writing code, or studying for an exam.

### Diffused Mode of Thinking
- This mode is used when your mind is **relaxed and not focused on one task**.
- It helps in **seeing the big picture and making creative connections**.
- Example: Getting ideas while walking, showering, or resting.

**Both modes are important**: active mode helps you work on problems, and diffused mode helps you find new ideas and solutions.

# What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Use the **Feynman Technique** to explain topics in simple words  
- Break topics into **small, manageable parts**  
- Switch between **focused (active)** and **relaxed (diffused)** thinking  
- Practice **active listening** while learning from others  
- Ask questions and clear doubts immediately  
- Revise regularly instead of last-minute studying  
- Apply what I learn through **practice or teaching others**  
- Remove distractions and create a **focused study environment**  
- Take short breaks to avoid mental fatigue  
- Track my progress and improve weak areas
