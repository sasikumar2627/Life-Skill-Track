## Sleep is your Superpower

1. Good sleep improves memory and learning ability.
2. Sleep helps the brain store and organize information.
3. Lack of sleep weakens the immune system.
4. Poor sleep increases the risk of heart disease, diabetes, and obesity.
5. Sleep affects mood and mental health.
6. Sleeping less than 6–7 hours regularly is harmful.
7. A consistent sleep routine improves overall health and performance.

## What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Create a Fixed Sleep Schedule
- Reduce Screen Time Before Bed
- Avoid Late Coffee
- Relax Before Sleeping
- Move Your Body
- Empty Your Mind

## Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise

- Exercise improves focus and attention
- Exercise strengthens memory and learning
- Exercise protects your brain long-term
- Exercise increases blood flow and oxygen to the brain, which helps your brain work better and think more clearly.
- It stimulates the growth of new brain cells and connections (neurogenesis), especially in areas like the hippocampus that are responsible for memory and learning.
- Regular exercise helps protect your brain from age-related decline and lowers the risk of diseases like Alzheimer’s and dementia.

## What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Start small
- Schedule time
- Make it easy
- Choose what You enjoy(Cycling, Walking, Running, Sports...)
- Get Morning Sun + Movement
- Find a friend
- Set Simple Goals(Both)
- Focus on Consistency, Not Perfection
